Cancellation and change terms

For stays booked for a specific date there is no right of withdrawal under Law No. 6502 art. 15/g. The terms below are demo examples.

  • event_available

    7+ days

    Free cancellation

    For cancellations up to 7 days before arrival the deposit is refunded.

  • nights_stay

    7–3 days

    First-night charge

    For cancellations between 7 and 3 days before arrival the first night is charged.

  • event_busy

    Under 3 days

    Full charge

    Cancellations within 3 days or no-shows are charged the full stay.

  • edit_calendar

    Changes

    Date changes

    Free up to 3 days before arrival, subject to availability.
